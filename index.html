With a small, walkable downtown and some hidden (and some not-so-hidden) gems, we encourage you to explore the area we’ve fallen in love with!

FAQs

Is this weekend kid-friendly?
▼
We love your sweet children and also want the weekend to be adult-forward, and some spaces (like the wood-fired lobster at the reception) are not kid-friendly. If you are not related to us and want to bring a child over 1 year old, please just reach out and we can chat!
Is there parking?
▼
There are 50 parking spots available at Marianmade Farm, so please carpool where possible and take shuttles on Saturday.
Will there be shuttles?
▼
Yes, we have two shuttles to take folks to the wedding on Saturday, and home from the reception at 10. We also have 2 taxis booked from 10pm–1am to take folks home from the after party.
What about Uber/Lyft?
▼
Wiscasset is a small town, and while you may have success hiring a ride, please do not count on this for time-sensitive transportation or late-night rides.
What is the dress code?
▼
We truly want you to wear what makes you feel fabulous! Hilary will be wearing red and asks that others don’t — the vision generally is “jewel tones by the water.” Think semi-dressed up Friday, dressed up Saturday, casual on Sunday!

Travel Information

A quick note
This area has a lot of great food, including lobster/seafood shacks (Red’s Eats, Sprague’s), ice cream (Blanchard’s Creamery, Round Top Ice Cream, Sweetz & More), and more sit-down dinner spots (Water Street Kitchen, Montsweag Roadhouse). There are great places for coffee & pastries (Wild Oats Bakery & Cafe, Treats), and oysters straight from the source (Glidden Point Oyster Farm, Eros Oyster Farm). If you’re looking to buy a little gift, stop by Westport Island Pottery (a quaint little shack of homemade pottery) or Rock Paper Scissors downtown. For a dip into nature, check out the Coastal Maine Botanical Gardens, Fort Edgecomb, or Chewonki’s Cushman Preserve.
Car Services, Shuttles & Rental Cars
Rental cars are the best way to get around coastal Maine, though there are also shuttles and buses from Portland if you’d prefer not to rent a car. On the wedding day we will have shuttles to take guests to and from the wedding, and taxis to take people home from the after party. Uber/Lyft may be possible, but is not reliable in this part of Maine.

As many people will be coming from Boston, Portland, and New York, we are creating a carpool spreadsheet for folks to fill out in case there are extra seats to be shared. If you are driving and open to taking more passengers, please fill this out!
